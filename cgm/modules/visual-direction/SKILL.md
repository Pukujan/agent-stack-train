# Visual direction

## Visual brief

Before generating, declare:

- asset role and audience;
- dominant message;
- required dimensions and crop behavior;
- primary subjects and their visibility requirements;
- palette, lighting, and contrast;
- text policy;
- reference assets;
- rejection conditions.

## Composition rules

- one dominant idea per image;
- keep the human/product subject visible and unobstructed;
- reserve negative space for layout text when required;
- use the hero as a reference, not as a reason to repeat the hero composition;
- use supporting assets to explain a different part of the story;
- keep diagrams simple enough to understand at the target size;
- do not use neon contrast or dense interface panels to manufacture complexity.

## Responsive roles

Declare separate roles for wide hero, wide problem, square system, portrait evidence, icon, and social preview. Do not force every asset into the hero's dimensions.
